FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Got questions? We're clear.

Is SoraPesa a bank or mobile money service?
No. SoraPesa is strictly a personal finance tracking and insight tool. We do not hold deposits, issue loans, or process monetary transactions.
Does SoraPesa read my SMS messages?
Never. We do not request or utilize Android SMS permissions. You maintain 100% control by manually uploading your exported M-Pesa PDF statements whenever you choose.
Is my financial data safe?
Yes. All imported data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Your numbers are never sold or shared with advertisers or third parties.
When is SoraPesa launching?
We are rolling out our early Android Beta in Kenya in Q3. Sign up via the waitlist above to receive early access.
Is it free?
The core statement parsing and salary breakdown features are completely free for early community members.
