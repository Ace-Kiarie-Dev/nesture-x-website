How to play Flappy Horror

Flappy Horror is a free Halloween arcade game you play right in your browser, on phone or desktop. You guide a little ghost through a graveyard of zombie hands. Each gap you slip through scores a point. Gather the lost souls floating in the gaps, phase through the dead when your meter is full, and survive the Blood Moon, when the hands speed up.

No sign-up and nothing to install. Your best score, souls and medals are saved in this browser.

Controls

  • Flap: press Space, click, or tap the screen.
  • Phase: press Shift or E, or tap the glowing orb, once 5 souls are collected.
  • Pause: P or Esc. Mute: M.
