CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who is Kikota for?

Built for gym owners, managers, and independent fitness clubs across Nairobi and Kenya looking for automated billing, check-ins, and regional competition.

Does Kikota work with M-Pesa?

Yes, natively built around automated M-Pesa STK push and instant reconciliation with zero manual paper recording or lost ledger entries.

Do my members need to download an app?

Members can view leaderboards and check in via progressive web app or web browser; no mandatory heavy app install required.

Is my gym's data shared with other gyms?

Your operations data (revenue, member lists, internal finances) is 100% private. Only opt-in tournament points and gym names appear on public leaderboards.

How much does it cost?

Transparent tiered monthly software plans based on active member count, with early bird locked-in pricing for founding gyms.

When is Kikota launching?

Private beta rollout begins next quarter in Nairobi, onboarding our first cohort of founding partner gyms.
